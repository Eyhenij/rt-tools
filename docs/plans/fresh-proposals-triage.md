# The taking apart of the fresh proposals of the intake

A plan of works, not a report: it says what is being done by the three proposals lying new in the
intake, in what order and why exactly in that one.

## Why

A proposal is a case the tree has already paid a session for: a miss happened, the rules layer did
not catch it, and the record about that left for the intake. While the record lies new, not a
single tree has the knowledge that was paid for: at the sender it lives as a review, at the rest it
lives nowhere.

The three records lying new now name three different cases, and they have one thing in common —
not one of them is closed by anything.

- **The fields of the kit work only with the former forms of Angular.** A field takes the control
  through `NgControl`, and a signal form of Angular 22 does not create it at all: the wrapper falls
  at the launch, and the subtree of the markup is not drawn. The price is named by a number at the
  side of the consumer: a long form is not divided into child components while the fields do not
  accept a signal form.
- **The law about the frontend application is silent about the browser storage.** A value put on
  the device has a kind — it belongs either to the device or to the one who signed in — and the
  kind decides whether the value is lifted at the leaving. The kind is not derived from the name,
  and it has no default.
- **The guard of the proposals reads the digest of the compaction as a request of the owner.** It
  arrives by their role and retells the turns that have already ended: a turn where not a word was
  said about the proposals is refused on a retelling of yesterday's request.

## Decisions

- **One epic for the three proposals.** The owner named them by one request; the order between them
  lives here, not in the memory of a session.
- **The work about the fields of the kit goes first.** Behind it stands the work of the consumer,
  and the other two are edits of a text and of a sign, and neither of them holds anybody.
- **The records of the neighbouring tree are not moved from here into "in work".** This is the rule
  of the taking apart of the cargo: "in work" is put on its own record by the tree it belongs to,
  and a foreign one is closed by the publisher straight into "ready".

## The order

The card of the epic is the task RT-1616; every line below is created by a task of its own.

| №           | Task                                                       | Why here                                                                                           |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 · RT-1617 | The fields of the kit accept a signal form                 | It holds the work of the consumer: the screen is not divided into components while the fields fall |
| 2 · RT-1618 | The kind of a value in the browser storage                 | An article of the law about the frontend: a rule of the tree stands under a law that is silent     |
| 3 · RT-1619 | A digest of the compaction is never a request of the owner | A sign in the guard of the proposals: a refused turn pays for a work nobody asked for              |

## The boundaries

- **A product agreement of its own is written in the task about the fields.** The edit touches the
  code of the kit, and the plan of the epic does not replace it. The other two works do not need
  one: there it is the text of a law and a sign of a guard.
- **The epic does not create a registry of the keys of the browser storage.** The article of the law
  names the kind of a value; by what the kind is declared in the tree is decided by a rule, and that
  is a work of its own.
- **The epic does not start a release of an edition of the package of the rules.** The publication
  is a decision of the owner of its own.

## What it ends with

The three records of the intake are moved into "ready" with the acceptance of the fixing, and the
owner is told in words: "the epic is over".
