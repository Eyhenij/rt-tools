## What is not in the epic

- **The release of a new edition of the package.** The edits land in the source; the publication is a
  decision of the owner of its own, and the skill of the edit of the source says that openly.
- **An edit of foreign trees.** What is true for any tree of this class leaves as a proposal into the
  intake and waits there for its taking apart.
- **The nine items already closed or created as tasks.** They are struck out of the files of the
  findings with a reference to their task, and are not taken apart anew.
- **The findings of the epic RT-733.** It is not over: of five tasks one is left, and its file of the
  findings is read together with it, not here.

## The sign of the closing

The epic is over when all six files of the findings are empty — in each of them not a single item is
left unsorted — and by every item it is visible what it ended with: the number of a task of this
epic, a reference to a sent proposal, or a line about it having fallen away and why.

It is checked by a listing of the items in the six files: a zero in each.

## The findings of this epic

They accumulate in a file of the findings next to this record — by the name common to the tree, from
the slug of the epic. The file is created by the first finding, not in advance.

been closed by works before the epic, the rest are brought together into fifteen subjects, and at
every item there stands a line about what it ended with — the number of a task, an edit of the
resources, or an argument why it fell away. The repetitions the epic was created for are closed once
each: the guard judging the paths from the body of a document — by the task RT-1201, the order of
