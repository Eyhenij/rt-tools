# The findings of the review of the family `reuse-first`

The role of the review of closed work read the family whole as the closing step of RT-2289: the
law, the rule under it, its pattern, the companion of the tree, the sign sets the rule points at,
and the two neighbouring rules the work collided with — `doc-style` and `task-flow`.

Fourteen findings came back: eight divergences between two texts, six cases no text named. Six of
them were closed inside the branch of RT-2289, because they lie in the very texts that work
edited. The other eight wait for the owner's word: they touch the pattern, the output of the check
and two neighbouring rules, and an edit there is neither this task's subject nor its size.

## Closed in the branch of RT-2289

1. **The rule declared uncounted what the package set counts.** The rewritten section named three
   articles no check sees, and the second of them — a template or styles inside the decorator — is
   a sign of the `angular` set. Now two articles stand there, and the third subject is named as
   counted.
2. **The pitfall steered a tree that writes its own kit away from declaring the set.** It knew two
   outcomes: declare, or do not, because you write the kit. The third one — declare with an area —
   is what the check supports and what this work took, and it was absent. That is the silence
   RT-2289 was opened to end, and the rule pointed straight into it. The article now names the
   area, with the shape of the record next to it.
3. **The area of a set is taught nowhere in the rules layer.** The pair of a name and roots lived
   in one spec of this tree and in one companion. It now stands in the rule, in the same article.
4. **The boundary of a sign stood only in the companion of this tree.** A whole piece drawn by
   hand in place of a ready-made component is named by no sign — a property of the mechanism, the
   same in every tree. It now stands in the rule, where the signs are described, and the law's
   article about stopping a departure is declared held in part.
5. **Declaring a set over code written before it is described nowhere.** Such a set lights up
   everything at once, and every place arrives as fresh rather than as debt. The new pitfall names
   both moves, the key that re-takes the snapshot included.
6. **The way to ask the uncounted numbers answered adjacent questions.** The first command counts
   the fields that inherit the base, that is the half that complies; the third answered none of the
   three articles. The section now holds two commands, and under each stands what its answer is a
   measurement of.

## Waiting for the owner

7. **The portable rule carries a tree's number and a tree's path.** The first table said "over
   seventy today" about the kit components and names the styles directory of an application. The
   number is removed in this branch; the package name and the path stay, and they are the first
   thing any reader of the rule sees.
8. **The pattern's command for "check that it is really missing" cannot run here.** It reads a
   barrel that does not exist in this tree and two library roots this tree does not have. Both
   commands answer with an error and emptiness, and emptiness reads as absence. The review names
   this as the plausible origin of the hand-drawn chat feed.
9. **The number that command yields answers another question.** A count of export lines does not
   say whether a component is in the kit.
10. **The marker of a departure: the rule makes it the owner's decision, the pattern lets the
    executor set it.** In the pattern the owner appears only in the neighbouring section about
    primitives, and the worked example justifies the marker with a task key. Following the
    pattern, an executor lifts a refusal on their own word.
11. **The flow graph routes the refused branch into the same end as the approved one.** Where the
    owner did not approve one's own primitive, the graph still ends in declaring the component.
    The marker appears in the graph nowhere at all.
12. **The word "sign" carries two meanings, and the summary line of the check uses the other
    one.** The line counts occurrences over files and calls them signs. That is what made the
    readiness of the first stage of RT-2289 read wrong.
13. **The partial declaration is described by nothing.** The pitfall knows only the case where not
    a single set is declared. A tree with one set declared and another missing gets silence on the
    screens of the second, and the same pitfall tells the reader to look whether the guard has
    anything to judge by — without naming a way to look.
14. **An article of the law has no rule line under it.** The article about repeated mechanics kept
    in one place is carried by a rule under a different law, and that law may be dropped by a
    tree. Nothing announces the hole.

Items 8 to 11 are edits of the pattern `reuse-first-extend`, item 12 an edit of the output of the
full check, item 14 a line in "What of the law is not here". They are written as proposals only
after the owner's word: a proposal sent unreviewed becomes the work of someone who did not order
it.
