# The remainder of the proposals created by the former order in the work queue

A plan of works, not a report: it says what is left to do by the proposals that arrived as cargo
before the rule of the taking apart of the cargo cancelled the creation of such records in the work
queue.

Gathered by the task RT-1490. Thirty-three tasks were read and reconciled with the tree across all
the branches of the storage; eight turned out closed by the editions 0.10–0.17 and were closed by a
comment, one is closed by an unmerged branch. Below is what is closed by nothing.

## How this was checked

The reconciling goes across all the branches of the storage, not by the working copy: the edits lie
in branches that wait for a merge, and a check by one copy calls what is done undone. A verbatim
coincidence of the ready text of a proposal with the tree is not an answer — the edit was accepted
by meaning, and a coincidence of six words in a row says only that the wording was taken whole.

## What to do with this

The works are divided by families: one branch per family, as the tasks of the epic RT-1198 went. The
order is from the top of the table down: the family of the delivery is the largest and the oldest,
and the work began with it.

The records of the cargo in the intake itself are not touched at that: foreign ones are marked only
by the sender, and one's own stand in "ready" and wait for the release of an edition.

## What this ended with

All four families are taken apart: the delivery — RT-1492, the conduct of the work — RT-1494, the
agreement — RT-1498, the harness — RT-1504. Of the thirty-three tasks of the remainder eight turned
out done earlier and were closed with an explanation, the rest are closed by the merge of the four
requests.

The foreign records of the cargo stayed new at that: the mark is accepted only by the tree that
sent it, and this was checked by a call — the intake answers "the tree has no such record". What to
do with a closed proposal of a neighbour is decided by the owner.
