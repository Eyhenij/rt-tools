# Law on the autonomous session

A session with no owner nearby. They answer no questions, press no buttons and do not see the
work until morning: everything the executor did overnight, they read at once and as already
happened.

## Articles

- **The owner declares an autonomous session, and it ends by their word or at a named boundary.**
  A session does not extend itself: work without an end is work the owner did not ask for.
- **A fork is closed by a default, not by waiting.** A question with nobody to ask does not stop
  the work: the executor takes a default, writes it down together with the cost of being wrong,
  and moves on. A standing session answers no question and costs exactly as much as one answered
  wrong.
- **A task that runs into the owner's word is postponed whole.** A half-done one looks done: the
  next session reads it as closed and does not come back to it.
- **An action visible from outside is not done in an autonomous session.** Sending to another
  party, publishing, merging, mail: their consequences are undone by a person, and there is none.
  Undoing costs more than waiting.
- **Work that cannot be confirmed by anything is not taken.** The readiness sign of an autonomous
  session is a command with a known output, not the executor's glance: there is nobody to confirm
  it.
- **Every step ends with a trace on disk.** A session breaks off without warning — on the window,
  on a refusal, on the network — and everything not written vanishes with it.
- **Pieces of work go in a chain, not in a fan from one base.** Each next one branches from the
  previous: a divergence then surfaces for whoever has both edits at hand, not for the owner,
  who has neither.
- **A session ends with a list, not a summary.** The owner reads the night whole: for every piece
  of work it shows what was done, where it lies, what confirms it and what it waits for. A
  summary "a lot was done" says nothing about any of the four.
- **A guard's refusal does not end an autonomous session.** It names the skipped step, and the
  step is done; a session that stopped on a refusal until morning loses the night whole.
