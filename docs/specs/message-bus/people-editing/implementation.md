# Creating, disabling and a new password — where the rules are carried out

The first column is the rule verbatim, as it is written in the "Rules" section of the spec. A rule
without a line and a line without a rule is a divergence: the spec promises what is not in the code,
or the code holds what the spec is silent about.

- **The three operations are closed by the right `accounts:manage`, and the read right does not open them.** — `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/accounts-manage.controller.ts:MANAGE_RIGHT` — one right, named once and put on all three operations
- **Creating refuses a taken name and leaves the existing record as it was.** — `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/accounts-manage.controller.ts:create` — the name is looked up by the key of `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/util/src/lib/account-name.util.ts:accountNameKey`. A found record is a conflict before anything is written
- **Creating and a new password refuse an empty password.** — `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/util/src/lib/person-edit.util.ts:passwordOf` — the pure decision. The word of the refusal is `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/util/src/lib/person-edit.util.ts:PERSON_EDIT_SAID`
- **Disabling cuts the live sign-ins of the record in the same transaction.** — `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/data-access/src/lib/account.queries.ts:disableAccount` — one transaction over the record and its sign-ins
- **The own record is not disabled.** — `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/accounts-manage.controller.ts:disable` — the signed-in person is compared by id. The screen does not draw the item for the own row: `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/feature/list/src/lib/admin-people-list.component.ts:selfName`
- **A disabled record is not disabled twice, and a name nobody has is refused as not found.** — `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/accounts-manage.controller.ts:#accountNamed` — the missing name; the second disabling is refused by the conflict in `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/accounts-manage.controller.ts:disable`
- **Every operation answers with the row of the list after the change.** — `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/data-access/src/lib/account.queries.ts:findPersonByNameKey` — the row is re-read after the write by `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/accounts-manage.controller.ts:#rowOf`
- **The button and the row menu are drawn only with `accounts:manage`.** — `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/feature/list/src/lib/admin-people-list.component.ts:canManage` — derived from the rights of `libs/message-bus-admin/auth/data-access/src/lib/auth.store.ts:AuthStore`. Until they are known nothing is hidden
- **The panel of creating lives at «people/new» in the outlet `ro`; the panel of a password at «people/<name>/password».** — `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/shell/src/lib/people.routes.ts:peopleRoutes` — the segments are `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/util/src/lib/person.columns.ts:PERSON_CREATE_ROUTE` and `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/util/src/lib/person.columns.ts:PERSON_PASSWORD_ROUTE`
- **A successful creating closes the panel and the list carries the new row at once.** — `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/feature/create-aside/src/lib/admin-person-create-aside.component.ts:submit` — `closeOnSuccess`. The list is re-read by `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/data-access/src/lib/people.store.ts:create`
- **A refused request keeps the person in the panel with their input, and the word of the receiver is shown as it is.** — `libs/message-bus-admin/common/core/util/src/lib/spoken-fault.ts:spokenFaultText` — shown by both panels. The shape of the refusal is read by `libs/message-bus-admin/common/core/api/src/lib/admin-read.api.ts:asSpokenFault`
- **Disabling asks a question that names the record and the consequence.** — `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/util/src/lib/person.logic.ts:personDisableQuestion` — the question lies in the row as a field for the confirmed menu item
- **The outcome of every action is said by one notification, and the list is re-read whole.** — `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/data-access/src/lib/people.store.ts:disable` — the notification bus and `retry` after every successful edit. The panels say theirs through `successText` of the shared base
