# Roles and rights on a screen — where the rules are carried out

The first column is the rule verbatim, as it is written in the "Rules" section of the spec. A rule
without a line and a line without a rule is a divergence: the spec promises what is not in the code,
or the code holds what the spec is silent about.

- **Everything about roles and about the access of a person is closed by the right `roles:manage`.** — `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/util/src/lib/role-edit.util.ts:ROLES_RIGHT` — one right, named once. It stands on every operation of `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/roles.controller.ts:RolesController` and of `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/accounts-access.controller.ts:AccountsAccessController`
- **A role is created by a name and a set of rights, and its key is derived from the name once.** — `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/roles.controller.ts:create` — the key is the name key of `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/util/src/lib/account-name.util.ts:accountNameKey`. The edit in `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/data-access/src/lib/role.queries.ts:updateRole` rewrites the name and the rights by the key, never the key itself
- **A role's name is unique the way a person's name is — by the key, not by the letters.** — `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/roles.controller.ts:#refuseTakenName` — the names of every role are read by `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/data-access/src/lib/role.queries.ts:listRoleNames` and compared by the key. The own key is left out at a rename
- **A right outside the closed set is refused, in a role and in a pointed edit alike.** — `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/util/src/lib/role-edit.util.ts:rightsFrom` — the pure parse. A name outside the set and a name given twice are refused before any write, from `roleInputOf` and `accessInputOf` alike
- **A role held by somebody is not deleted.** — `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/roles.controller.ts:remove` — the count of people comes with the role from `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/data-access/src/lib/role.queries.ts:findRoleByKey`, and a count above zero is a conflict naming it
- **The access of a person is replaced whole: the role and all the pointed edits in one transaction.** — `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/data-access/src/lib/role.queries.ts:replaceAccountAccess` — one transaction: the role reference, the removal of the former edits and the write of the new ones
- **An edit that would leave the signed-in person without `roles:manage` is refused with the reason.** — `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/util/src/lib/role-edit.util.ts:keepsRolesRight` — the pure decision over the role rights and the edits. It is asked by `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/roles.controller.ts:replace` for the own role and by `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/accounts-access.controller.ts:#refuseLockOut` for the own record
- **A role and a person nobody has are refused as not found.** — `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/roles.controller.ts:#roleNamed` — the role; `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/accounts-access.controller.ts:#accessNamed` — the person. A role named in the access that does not exist is refused by `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/accounts-access.controller.ts:#roleWanted`
- **Every operation answers with the record after the edit.** — `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/feature/src/lib/roles.controller.ts:#roleNamed` — the role is re-read after the write. The access is re-read by `libs/message-bus-api/accounts/data-access/src/lib/role.queries.ts:findAccountAccess` after the transaction
- **The item "Роли" and the address of the section are closed by `roles:manage`, by the one declaration of the menu.** — `libs/message-bus-admin/common/container/util/src/lib/menu.declaration.ts:ADMIN_MENU` — the right stands at the item, and the route guard reads it from there
- **A row names the role, its rights in words and how many people hold it.** — `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/util/src/lib/role.mapper.ts:RoleShortMapper` — the rights in words come from `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/util/src/lib/right.labels.ts:rightsLabel`, and an empty set says so by the dictionary word
- **The button of creating and the row menu are drawn only with the right.** — `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/feature/roles-list/src/lib/admin-roles-list.component.ts:canManage` — derived from the rights of the sign-in store; until they are known nothing is hidden
- **The panel of a role lives at «roles/new» and «roles/<key>» in the outlet `ro`.** — `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/shell/src/lib/roles.routes.ts:rolesRoutes` — the segment of creating is `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/util/src/lib/role.columns.ts:ROLE_CREATE_ROUTE`. The panel reads the role by the key from the address in `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/feature/role-aside/src/lib/admin-role-aside.component.ts:resolve`
- **The rights in the panel are grouped by section, one checkbox per right.** — `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/util/src/lib/right.labels.ts:rightGroups` — the groups are derived from the closed set by the section of every right, in the order of the set
- **The item "Удалить" is drawn only for a role nobody holds, and it asks a question naming the role.** — `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/util/src/lib/role.logic.ts:roleCanDelete` — the sign lies in the row. The question is `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/util/src/lib/role.logic.ts:roleDeleteQuestion`, given to the confirmed menu item
- **A successful edit closes the panel, one notification says the outcome, and the list is re-read whole.** — `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/feature/role-aside/src/lib/admin-role-aside.component.ts:submit` — `closeOnSuccess`. The list is re-read by `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/data-access/src/lib/roles.store.ts:RolesStore` after every successful call. The refused one stays in the panel with the word of `libs/message-bus-admin/common/core/util/src/lib/spoken-fault.ts:spokenFaultText`
- **The item "Права" of the people list row is drawn only with `roles:manage`.** — `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/feature/list/src/lib/admin-people-list.component.ts:canGrant` — its own sign next to the right of editing people. The menu button itself is drawn by `showRowActions` when either is held
- **The panel of access lives at «people/<name>/access» in the outlet `ro` and reads the access by the name from the address.** — `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/shell/src/lib/people.routes.ts:peopleRoutes` — the segment is `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/util/src/lib/role.columns.ts:PERSON_ACCESS_ROUTE`. The panel reads the access and the roles together in `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/feature/access-aside/src/lib/admin-person-access-aside.component.ts:resolve`
- **A right the role says nothing about shows "нет" while its word is "По роли".** — `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/util/src/lib/role.logic.ts:accessOutcome` — the pure addition of the role rights and the words, the same as the receiver's. The words themselves come from the edits by `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/util/src/lib/role.logic.ts:accessWordsOf`
- **The outcome follows the choice at once, before the save.** — `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/feature/access-aside/src/lib/admin-person-access-aside.component.ts:outcome` — a derived value over the role choice and the words of the form
- **A successful save closes the panel and re-reads the people list.** — `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/data-access/src/lib/people.store.ts:replaceAccess` — the re-read after the answer. The panel closes by `closeOnSuccess` in `libs/message-bus-admin/accounts/feature/access-aside/src/lib/admin-person-access-aside.component.ts:submit`
