# The intake of the cargo — where the rules are carried out

The first column is the rule verbatim, as it is written in the "Rules" section of the spec. A rule
without a line and a line without a rule is a divergence: the spec promises what is not in the code,
or the code holds what the spec is silent about.

- **The form of the cargo is declared by one place, and the intake does not redeclare it.** — `libs/message-bus-common/src/lib/cargo.ts:IIntakeResponse`
- **Cargo of every kind is taken in by an operation of its own.** — `libs/message-bus-api/observations/feature/src/lib/summary-intake.controller.ts:accept`
- **The intake does not take the content of the digest apart.** — `libs/message-bus-api/observations/util/src/lib/summary.const.ts:SUMMARY_FIELDS`
- **The version of the schema of the cargo is mandatory, and the versions of the schema of the rows of the observations are a part of the content.** — `libs/message-bus-common/src/lib/cargo-shape.ts:cargoSchemaOf`
- **Cargo of an unknown version of the schema is taken in and marked by it.** — `libs/message-bus-api/observations/data-access/src/lib/month-record.queries.ts:writeMonthSummary`
- **Cargo heavier than the limit is not taken in.** — `apps/message-bus/src/app/cargo-limit.ts:cargoLimit`
- **A tree introduces itself by a token, and the intake holds only its hash.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/util/src/lib/tree-token.util.ts:treeTokenHash`
- **The token is printed once, at the issuing.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/util/src/lib/tree-report.util.ts:tokenIssuedLines`
- **A tree has one valid token: a new one replaces the former one, marking it as revoked.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/data-access/src/lib/tree.queries.ts:replaceTreeToken`
- **A revoked token stops being accepted at once.** — `libs/message-bus-api/access/feature/src/lib/access.guard.ts:canActivate`
- **A revoked token is not deleted.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/data-access/src/lib/tree.queries.ts:revokeTreeTokens`
- **The sign of a tree is named at the creating, it is not taken from the first cargo.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/data-access/src/lib/tree.queries.ts:createTreeWithToken`
- **The sign of the tree in the cargo is checked against the tree of the token.** — `libs/message-bus-common/src/lib/cargo-fault.ts:cargoFault`
- **The creating and the revocation of a token of a tree are not done by an operation of a request.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/feature/src/lib/tree-commands.service.ts:run`
- **A record of a month is one per pair "tree — month".** — `libs/message-bus-api/observations/data-access/src/lib/month-record.queries.ts:ensureMonthRecord`
- **The digest of the last run replaces the former one whole.** — `libs/message-bus-api/observations/data-access/src/lib/month-record.queries.ts:writeMonthSummary`
- **The proposals and the incident analyses pile up, they are not replaced.** — `libs/message-bus-api/proposals/data-access/src/lib/proposal.queries.ts:addProposals`
- **A record of a month remembers the time of the last run.** — `libs/message-bus-api/observations/data-access/src/lib/month-record.queries.ts:IMonthRecordInput`
- **The month is taken by the clock of the intake, in universal time.** — `libs/message-bus-common/src/lib/month.ts:monthOf`
- **The observations of the past month that arrived by the first run of the new one land in the new one.** — `libs/message-bus-common/src/lib/month.ts:monthOf`
- **The simultaneous arrival of two runs of one tree is resolved by the storage, not by a check by reading.** — `libs/message-bus-api/observations/data-access/src/lib/month-record.queries.ts:ensureMonthRecord`
- **The pair "tree — month" is unique.** — `prisma/schema.prisma:MonthRecord`
- **Cargo that did not pass the check of the form is refused whole within its own operation.** — `libs/message-bus-common/src/lib/cargo-fault.ts:cargoFaultMessage`
- **All the records of one operation land together or do not land at all.** — `libs/message-bus-api/postmortems/data-access/src/lib/postmortem.queries.ts:writePostmortems`
- **The proposals and the analyses that arrived before the digest create the record of a month themselves.** — `libs/message-bus-api/proposals/feature/src/lib/proposals-intake.controller.ts:accept`
- **A refusal of the intake names the reason to the tree, not the details of its own workings.** — `apps/message-bus/src/app/failure.filter.ts:catch`
- **Every refusal is written into the journal of the intake with the kind of the cargo and the sign of the tree.** — `apps/message-bus/src/app/failure.filter.ts:FailureFilter`
- **The intake gives nothing without a token of a tree.** — `libs/message-bus-api/access/util/src/lib/operation-access.ts:OPERATION_ACCESS`
- **The probe of liveness answers only when the storage answers too.** — `apps/message-bus/src/app/health/health.controller.ts:check`
- **The probe of liveness names neither the edition nor the composition — neither in the body nor in the headers of the answer.** — `apps/message-bus/src/main.ts:serve`
- **An incident analysis arrives as text whole.** — `libs/message-bus-api/postmortems/util/src/lib/postmortem.const.ts:POSTMORTEM_ITEM_FIELDS`
- **The check for the address of a tree does not cover an incident analysis.** — `projects/agent-kit/src/lib/shipment.ts:leaksOfCargo`
- **An analysis is recognised by the name of its file at the tree.** — `libs/message-bus-api/postmortems/data-access/src/lib/postmortem.queries.ts:IPostmortemRow`
- **An analysis that arrived a second time updates the former one, it does not create a second one.** — `libs/message-bus-api/postmortems/data-access/src/lib/postmortem.queries.ts:writePostmortems`
- **An analysis that disappeared at the tree stays at the intake.** — `libs/message-bus-api/postmortems/feature/src/lib/postmortems-intake.controller.ts:accept`
- **A proposal is recognised by a sign — the hash of its text, not by the text in a constraint.** — `libs/message-bus-api/proposals/util/src/lib/proposal.logic.ts:proposalDigest`
- **The sign of a proposal is unique within the tree, not within the record of a month.** — `prisma/schema.prisma:Proposal`
- **What already lay there is skipped, it does not refuse the request.** — `libs/message-bus-api/proposals/data-access/src/lib/proposal.queries.ts:addProposals`
- **The answer of the intake to the proposals names how many records landed and how many already lay there.** — `libs/message-bus-api/proposals/feature/src/lib/proposals-intake.controller.ts:accept`
- **A proposal stays at its record of a month.** — `libs/message-bus-api/proposals/feature/src/lib/proposals-intake.controller.ts:accept`
- **A proposal keeps the resource it belongs to.** — `libs/message-bus-api/proposals/util/src/lib/proposal.logic.ts:PROPOSAL_ITEM_FIELDS`
- **A token is issued only by a valid invitation.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/util/src/lib/tree-invite.util.ts:inviteUsable`
- **The invitation goes out in the same record of the storage the token is released by.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/data-access/src/lib/tree-invite.queries.ts:redeemInvite`
- **The name of the tree is set by the owner at the issuing of the invitation, not by the request.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/feature/src/lib/enroll.controller.ts:enroll`
- **A sign of a tree that is already created is not recreated by a request.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/data-access/src/lib/tree.queries.ts:findTreeClash`
- **An invitation is valid for a limited time.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/util/src/lib/tree-invite.util.ts:inviteExpiry`
- **A request for a token is a public operation creating a record, and it is closed by a limiter of the frequency.** — `libs/message-bus-api/access/feature/src/lib/rate-limit.service.ts:allow`
- **Only the hash of the invitation lies in the storage.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/util/src/lib/tree-invite.util.ts:inviteCodeHash`
- **The token goes away to the tree by the single answer to the request.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/feature/src/lib/enroll.controller.ts:enroll`
- **The request goes only over a protected connection.** — `projects/agent-kit/src/lib/enroll.ts:intakeAllowed`
- **Neither the invitation nor the token gets into the observations and the logs.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/feature/src/lib/enroll.controller.ts:enroll`
- **The package does not overwrite a token that already lies there without asking.** — `projects/agent-kit/src/lib/enroll.ts:enroll`
- **There is one valid invitation per name, and it is held by the storage.** — `prisma/schema.prisma:TreeInvite`
- **The reference to the created tree is filled in at the minute of the using up.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/data-access/src/lib/tree-invite.queries.ts:redeemInvite`
- **The invitation is issued by two — the command of the node and the operation of the admin application — and they decide one and the same.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/feature/src/lib/invite-issue.ts:issueInvite`
- **A taken name refuses the issuing whole.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/feature/src/lib/invite-issue.ts:issueInvite`
- **The code goes away to whoever called the issuing, and nowhere else.** — `libs/message-bus-api/trees/feature/src/lib/invites-read.controller.ts:issue`
- **A state is at an incident analysis and at a proposal, and a digest of a month has none.** — `prisma/schema.prisma:Postmortem`
- **There are four states, and there are no others.** — `libs/message-bus-common/src/lib/cargo-state.ts:ECargoState`
- **The state comes as an enumeration, not as a string at the place of the use.** — `libs/message-bus-common/src/lib/cargo-state.ts:cargoStateOf`
- **A record that arrived stands in "new".** — `prisma/schema.prisma:CargoState`
- **The records that arrived before the creating of the field are read as new.** — `prisma/migrations/20260820103518_cargo_record_state/migration.sql:CargoState`
- **An arrival that changed the text of an analysis brings it back into "new".** — `libs/message-bus-api/postmortems/util/src/lib/postmortem-arrival.util.ts:postmortemArrivalUpdate`
- **An arrival that did not change the text does not touch the state.** — `libs/message-bus-api/postmortems/util/src/lib/postmortem-arrival.util.ts:postmortemArrivalUpdate`
- **The intake of the cargo does not accept a state.** — `libs/message-bus-api/postmortems/util/src/lib/postmortem.const.ts:POSTMORTEM_ITEM_FIELDS`
