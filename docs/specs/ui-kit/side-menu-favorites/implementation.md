# What it is carried out by — favourites of the side menu

The rule of the subdomain is on the left, the place where it is carried out is on the right. The paths are
given from the root of the tree.

- **Favourites are switched on by providing the service, and without it the menu is as before.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/rtui-side-menu-favorites.component.ts:favorites`, `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.ts:favorites`
- **Favourites are switched on per strip item by its `favorites` flag, and it is off by default.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/favorites.logic.ts:favoritesSection`, the stars — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.ts:favoritesOn`
- **The block of a section shows only the favourites of that section.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/rtui-side-menu-favorites.component.ts:rows`
- **The section is the one whose submenu the panel shows, and an empty panel has none.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu/rtui-side-menu.component.ts:shownSubMenu`, the lookup — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/favorites.logic.ts:favoritesSection`
- **The section is recognised by its items' ids as well, not only by the array.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/favorites.logic.ts:favoritesSection`
- **The settings are held by the service, and the application reads and writes them through the same service.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/rtui-side-menu-settings.service.ts:RtuiSideMenuSettingsService`
- **Each menu keeps its settings under its own id, and the application names the id.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu/rtui-side-menu.component.ts:menuId`, the empty id — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/side-menu-settings.logic.ts:normalizeMenuId`, `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/rtui-side-menu-settings.service.ts:settings`, the fields kept — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/side-menu-settings.logic.ts:normalizeSettings`
- **The service is provided once, in the application's environment injector, and the menu reads it optionally.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/rtui-side-menu-settings.service.ts:provideRtuiSideMenuSettings`
- **The settings are kept in the browser storage under a key the consumer may name.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/rtui-side-menu-settings.service.ts:#storage`, the key by default — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/side-menu-settings.logic.ts:SIDE_MENU_SETTINGS_KEY`
- **A write reads the storage first and changes one field of one menu.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/rtui-side-menu-settings.service.ts:#update`, the patch — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/side-menu-settings.logic.ts:patchSettings`
- **A change made by another tab reaches the signals by the storage event.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/rtui-side-menu-settings.service.ts:#fresh`, the listener in the constructor of `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/rtui-side-menu-settings.service.ts:RtuiSideMenuSettingsService`
- **The kit deletes no settings by itself.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/rtui-side-menu-settings.service.ts:deleteSettings`, called by nothing in the kit
- **A broken record in the storage reads as no settings, and a failed write keeps them in memory.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/side-menu-settings.logic.ts:readSettings`, `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/side-menu-settings.logic.ts:normalizeSettings`, `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/rtui-side-menu-settings.service.ts:#commit`
- **Only ids are kept; the row is built from the menu's own items.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/favorites.logic.ts:findFavoriteItems`
- **An id the menu does not have is not shown, and it stays in the list.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/favorites.logic.ts:findFavoriteItems`
- **A star stands on every item with an address of its own in a favourites section, folders excluded.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/favorites.logic.ts:isFavoriteCandidate`, the markup — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.html:side-menu-favorite-star`
- **A row of the block carries a remove button, not a star.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.ts:onRemoveFavorite`, the markup — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.html:side-menu-favorite-remove`
- **An item in the list carries a filled star, the rest a hollow one.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.html:star_border`
- **The colour of the filled star is the theme's, and the application sets any other.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.scss:rt-side-menu-favorite-color`
- **Every colour, size and spacing of favourites is a property the application overrides.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/rtui-side-menu-favorites.component.scss:rt-side-menu-favorites-divider-gap`, `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.scss:rt-side-menu-favorite-action-size`
- **The hollow star and the remove button show on hover and on focus inside their row; the filled star always shows.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.scss:__favorite`
- **The star carries a tooltip and an accessible name: "Add to favourites" or "Remove from favourites".** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.html:matTooltip`
- **A press of the star switches the favourite and does nothing else.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.ts:onToggleFavorite`
- **A keyboard focus on a star or a remove button holds a submenu opened by hover until it closes; a mouse press does not.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.ts:onFavoriteFocus`, the hold — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu/rtui-sub-menu-hold.service.ts:RtuiSubMenuHoldService`
- **After a remove, the focus stands on the remove button of the neighbouring row.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.ts:onRemoveFavorite`
- **The block stands at the top of the submenu of a favourites section, under the search field and above the list.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu/rtui-side-menu.component.html:rtui-side-menu-favorites`
- **The block shows the favourites in the order of the list.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/rtui-side-menu-favorites.component.ts:rows`
- **The block with nothing to show takes no place.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/rtui-side-menu-favorites.component.html:rows`
- **While the search query is not empty, the block is hidden.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/rtui-side-menu-favorites.component.ts:rows`
- **A row of the block opens its item the same way as the row in the list does.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu/rtui-side-menu.component.ts:onClickSubMenu`
- **A row of the block is marked active by the same rule as the row in the list.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.html:activeMenuIds`
- **A row of the block carries no page id of its item and no keyboard ring.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.ts:inFavorites`
- **A row of the block is dragged by its handle, and the new order is kept at the drop.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/rtui-side-menu-favorites.component.ts:onDrop`, the handle — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/rtui-side-menu-favorites.component.html:handle-icon`
- **A drop moves the dragged id next to its visible neighbour, and hidden ids keep their places.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/favorites.logic.ts:moveVisibleFavorite`, for the application — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/rtui-side-menu-settings.service.ts:moveVisible`
- **While a row is dragged, the submenu stays open in either mode, and the drop releases it.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/rtui-side-menu-favorites.component.ts:onDragStart`, the release — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/rtui-side-menu-favorites.component.ts:onDragEnd`, the hold — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu/rtui-sub-menu-hold.service.ts:RtuiSubMenuHoldService`, the drop outside the panel — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/favorites.logic.ts:isDroppedOutside`
- **The chevron of a folder in a favourites section stands in the column of the stars.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.html:favoritesOn`
- **The arrows on a handle move its row to the neighbouring place, and the focus goes with it.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/rtui-side-menu-favorites.component.ts:onHandleKey`
- **A dropped row outside the block changes nothing.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/rtui-side-menu-favorites.component.ts:onDrop`
- **The block does not keep open a pinned panel that has nothing else to show.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu/rtui-side-menu.component.ts:subMenuOpened`
- **The labels are sewn in in English and are replaced by the provider settings.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/rtui-side-menu-settings.service.ts:labels`
- **The icons of the remove button and the handle are replaced by the provider settings, one without the other.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/rtui-side-menu-settings.service.ts:icons`, the blank glyph — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/settings/rtui-side-menu-settings.service.ts:#icon`, the markup — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.html:favorite-icon`
- **The remove icon turns the danger colour under the pointer and the keyboard focus.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.scss:rt-side-menu-favorite-remove-hover-color`, the mark — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.html:remove: true`
- **On a narrow screen the block stands the same, under the search of the submenu.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu/rtui-side-menu.component.html:rtui-side-menu-favorites`
- **An item flagged `favoriteDisabled` is not a favourite candidate.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/favorites.logic.ts:isFavoriteCandidate`, the block — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/favorites.logic.ts:findFavoriteItems`, the star — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu-sub-item/rtui-side-menu-sub-item.component.html:favoriteDisabled`
- **The heading of the block is a toggle that collapses the block and expands it.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/rtui-side-menu-favorites.component.ts:onToggleCollapsed`, the markup — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/rtui-side-menu-favorites.component.html:aria-controls`
- **The menu input `favoritesCount` decides when the heading shows the number of rows.** — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/favorites/rtui-side-menu-favorites.component.ts:countShown`, the input — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/menu/rtui-side-menu.component.ts:favoritesCount`, the unknown value — `projects/ui-kit/src/lib/ui-kit/side-menu/side-menu.logic.ts:normalizeFavoritesCount`
