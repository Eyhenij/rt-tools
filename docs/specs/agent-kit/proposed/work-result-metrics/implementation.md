# Binding — the metrics of the work result

A statement of the spec and the place where it is carried out. The link goes by the text of the
statement: a removed statement is removed together with its line. Statements with no line yet are
carried out by later stages of the work.

- **Calls, refusals, the owner's words, interrupts and re-asks are counted from the session record, not from the observations.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-count.mjs:eventsOf` — the events of the entries of a record; scenario `SC-AK-1612`
- **The hook at the end of a turn counts, and it writes observations.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/hooks/record-turn.sh:counter` — on Stop and SessionEnd; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-turn.mjs:observationsOf` — one line per event, by the day of its moment; scenario `SC-AK-1611`
- **The hook keeps a mark per session.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/hooks/record-turn.sh:marks` — the mark `record-<sign>.json` in the marks directory; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-turn.mjs:readFrom` — the offset read; scenario `SC-AK-1641`
- **An unfinished last line of the record waits for the next turn.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-turn.mjs:readFrom` — the offset stops at the last whole line; scenario `SC-AK-1641`
- **A broken line in the middle of the record is skipped and counted.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-turn.mjs:readFrom` — the skipped count; the observation `record-skip`; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/work-result.mjs:linesOf` — the report names the count with its day; scenario `SC-AK-1642`
- **The stop refusal of a turn is counted by the next count.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/hooks/record-turn.sh:record` — the SessionEnd count reads the line written after the stop hooks; scenario `SC-AK-1643`
- **The counting never fails the turn.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-turn.mjs` — every path exits with zero, a read that broke writes `record-broken`; scenario `SC-AK-1629`
- **An observation keeps the moment of each call and each refusal, with its class, check and branch.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-turn.mjs:observationsOf` — the moment, the kind, the check and the task key of the branch; scenario `SC-AK-1611`
- **The cut is rebuilt from the full record, and it keeps what the counter reads.** — `projects/agent-kit/tests/real-data/cut-record.mjs:cutEntry` — the session, the branch, the queued messages, the opening of a refusal and the re-ask sign; scenario `SC-AK-1611`
- **The owner's words are counted per message, and a wrapper of the harness is never counted.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-count.mjs:typedOf` — a typed or a queued message of a person; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-count.mjs:ownerWords` — a queued message repeated by a typed one counts once; scenarios `SC-AK-1610`, `SC-AK-1650`
- **Owner corrections are matched among the owner's words.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-count.mjs:corrections` — the set of patterns of the hook that declares it; scenario `SC-AK-1620`
- **Pairs of refusals are counted by the ordered pair of checks.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-count.mjs:pairsOf` — two different checks with no successful call between; scenario `SC-AK-1614`
- **Idle time is counted in whole minutes per stretch, and a stretch belongs to the day it started.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-count.mjs:idleOf` — from a refusal to the next change of the tree; scenarios `SC-AK-1616`, `SC-AK-1617`, `SC-AK-1648`
- **A re-ask is told by the beginning of the question.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-count.mjs:isReask` — the fixed set `REASK` over the first `REASK_SPAN` characters; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-count.mjs:reasksOf` — after an answered menu question of the same task; scenario `SC-AK-1619`
- **An interrupt is counted per entry.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-count.mjs:eventsOf` — the event `interrupt`; scenario `SC-AK-1620`
- **A past day is counted by a one-off recount of the saved session records, by the same counter.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-recount.mjs:recount` — a day the hook counted is left, an earlier recount of the day is replaced; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-recount.mjs:recordsOf` — the records of the tree by the working directory or by the branches, read line by line; scenario `SC-AK-1632`
- **A merged task is counted on the day of its merge, with the minutes from its taking.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/merge-read.mjs:dayMerges` — the merges of the day and the minutes from the earliest taking; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-turn.mjs:takingsOf` — the first entry of a session on the branch of a task; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/merge-read.mjs:mergesOf` — one observation per merged request; scenarios `SC-AK-1622`, `SC-AK-1636`
- **An epic is judged by the end of the day of the report, not by today.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/merge-read.mjs:dayEpics` — only what happened by the end of the day is read; scenarios `SC-AK-1623`, `SC-AK-1624`
- **An epic carries the minutes from its taking to its release, and to its merge while not released.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/merge-read.mjs:dayEpics` — the earliest taking of the epic branch or of a task merged into it; scenario `SC-AK-1639`
- **A tree that publishes no package prints its epics as merged, never as released.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/merge-read.mjs:dayEpics` — the status `merged`; scenario `SC-AK-1652`
- **An epic that changes several packages is released when each of them has an edition after the merge.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/merge-read.mjs:dayEpics` — an edition after the merge per package, an epic with no package is released by its merge; scenarios `SC-AK-1623`, `SC-AK-1651`
- **Merges and releases are read by the outcome read of the sending, from the hosting and the package registry, with their moments.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/hooks/pull-merges.sh:rt_merge_facts` — the requests from the tree profile, the editions from the registry, the day of the last read in its own mark; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/merge-read.mjs:writeFacts` — a fact read again replaces the first; scenario `SC-AK-1622`
- **An epic window closes at the first of three events.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/epic-window.mjs:epicWindows` — the next merge into the epic branch, the merge of the epic, the analysis turn; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/epic-window.mjs:analysisStartsOf` — the owner message that opens an analysis turn after the session left the epic branch; scenarios `SC-AK-1633`, `SC-AK-1615`
- **A window counts only the sessions that stood on the epic branch or the branch of a task of the epic within the window.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/epic-window.mjs:epicTasksOf` — the epic and the tasks merged into its branch; scenario `SC-AK-1634`
- **An epic window opens at the merge of a task request into the epic branch.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/merge-read.mjs:epicBranchesOf` — a base that is no task branch and no main branch; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/merge-read.mjs:mergesOf` — the mark `opens` on such a merge; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/epic-window.mjs:epicWindows` — a merge after the epic itself merged opens nothing; scenarios `SC-AK-1636`, `SC-AK-1655`
- **The epics of the period are every epic with a window or a merge in the period, open ones included.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/work-result.mjs:periodEpics` — the windows, the merges and the releases inside the period, the status by the end of its last day; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/work-result.mjs:epicLines` — a line per epic and per window, an open window as «открыто»; scenarios `SC-AK-1623`, `SC-AK-1639`
- **A role verdict «goal not met» counts against the task of its moment, and «частично достигнута» is «goal not met».** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/hooks/role-calls.sh:rt_verdict_task` — the key of the branch, on the main branch the epic of the copy or `none`; `projects/agent-kit/assets/hooks/role-calls.sh:rt_refusal_words` — «частично» among the refusal words; scenarios `SC-AK-1621`, `SC-AK-1640`
- **Refusals per hundred calls are counted by day and by check, against the calls of the day.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/work-result.mjs:dayResult` — the refusals of a day and of each check against the calls of the day, two decimals; scenario `SC-AK-1613`
- **«Задачи слиты» is not «Принято с первого раза».** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/work-result.mjs:linesOf` — «Задачи слиты» from the merges of the day; `projects/agent-kit/src/lib/outcomes.ts:outcomeLines` — «принято с первого раза» from the outcomes of requests; scenario `SC-AK-1636`
- **The lines of the result are listed, and none of them prints «нет данных».** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/work-result.mjs:linesOf` — every line prints a number; `projects/agent-kit/src/lib/metrics.ts:NOT_RECORDED_YET` — the lines of the result are not in it; scenario `SC-AK-1627`
- **«Принято с первого раза» with no task prints «0 из 0», in the report and on the screen alike.** — `projects/agent-kit/src/lib/work-result.ts:FIRST_PASS_NONE` — put in when the outcomes have no main number; scenario `SC-AK-1627`
- **A day with no session record says so once, as a sentence, and prints the merges of that day.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/work-result.mjs:dayResult` — a day with no record carries no number of the record, merges and verdicts stay; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/work-result.mjs:linesOf` — one sentence per such day; scenario `SC-AK-1628`
- **A day older than the counting and not recounted is named apart.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/work-result.mjs:firstCountedDay` — the first day the hook counted; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/work-result.mjs:linesOf` — «запись дня до счётчика»; scenario `SC-AK-1649`
- **Milestone G is reached on a day with fewer than five refusals per hundred calls and no dead end.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/work-result.mjs:milestoneOf` — under `MILESTONE_G_LIMIT` and no dead end, a day with no call prints «вызовов нет»; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/work-result.mjs:linesOf` — one line per day; scenarios `SC-AK-1626`, `SC-AK-1637`, `SC-AK-1638`
- **«Повторы ошибок» says «считается в приёмнике».** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/work-result.mjs:linesOf` — the last line of the result; scenario `SC-AK-1627`
- **A dead end is a series the executor could not leave.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/record-count.mjs:deadEndsOf` — `DEAD_END_SERIES` refusals of one check within `DEAD_END_GAP_MS`; scenario `SC-AK-1625`
