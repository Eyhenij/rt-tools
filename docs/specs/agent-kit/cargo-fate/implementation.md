# Binding — the fate of one's own records in the intake

A statement of the spec and the place where it is carried out. The link goes by the text of the
statement: a removed statement is removed together with its line.

- **The fate of its own records is asked by a command of the tree, not by a person's sign-in.** — `projects/agent-kit/src/lib/fate.ts:fate`
- **The reading is closed by the token of the tree — the same one the send goes under.** — `projects/agent-kit/src/lib/ship.ts:httpReadOwn`
- **Every record is shown with its state, what it was fixed by and the version of the release.** — `projects/agent-kit/src/lib/fate.ts:recordLine`
- **An override is tied to a record by the mark of its section, not by a guess from the resource name.** — `projects/agent-kit/src/lib/fate.ts:markOf`
- **The lifting of a section is advised by the laid-out edition, not by the word of the intake.** — `projects/agent-kit/src/lib/override-marks.ts:staleOverrides`
- **A released record whose article the laid-out edition does not hold is named as an update, not as a lifting.** — `projects/agent-kit/src/lib/fate.ts:markLine`
- **The command lifts nothing itself.** — `projects/agent-kit/src/lib/fate.ts:fate`
- **A mark whose record the intake does not give out is named apart, not dropped.** — `projects/agent-kit/src/lib/fate.ts:orphanLines`
- **A missing token or intake address is refused before the network, and the refusal names where they live.** — `projects/agent-kit/src/lib/fate.ts:missingWay`

The names of this tree: the token lies in the file named by the key `token` of the tree settings,
the intake address by the key `intake`, and the overrides in `.claude/rt-kit/overrides/`.
