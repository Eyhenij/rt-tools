statement: a removed statement is removed together with its line.

- **A word of the owner that asks to stop or to wait, typed or chosen in a menu, releases no turn that wrote while the scenario is open.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/owner.mjs:interrupted` — only the mark of Esc is read as the owner's stop; `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/source/owner.mjs:wordOf` — a typed message or the options chosen in a menu; scenarios `SC-AK-1457`, `SC-AK-1458`, `SC-AK-1460`, `SC-AK-1548`
- **A session that wrote nothing releases the turn on a question or on swearing of the owner.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/session.mjs:wrote` — an edit or a command of the work; scenarios `SC-AK-1456`, `SC-AK-1459`
- **The end of the epic releases the turn: every task lies in the epic branch, and the PR of the epic into the main branch exists.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/epic.mjs:finished` — the tips of the tasks and the PR of the epic; scenario `SC-AK-1462`
- **A menu of a refusal held twice in a row releases the turn; held once, it does not.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/session.mjs:menuLost` — the streak of menus that did not reach the owner; scenarios `SC-AK-1455`, `SC-AK-1470`
- **The menu hook lets the menu of the exit check through and holds an option that removes or weakens a check.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/hooks/ask-judge.md:weakens` — the case that holds such an option in any menu, next to the case that lets the menu of the exit check leave; scenarios `SC-AK-1455`, `SC-AK-1470`
- **The model hook on Stop does not judge open work and holds a question whose answer is already given.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/hooks/turn-judge.md:already` — the case of a question already answered, under the section that leaves open work to the exit check; scenario `SC-AK-1451`
- **A recorded turn carries no text the owner typed: the word is a class label or a phrase of at most four words that asks to stop or to wait.** — `projects/agent-kit/tests/turn-replay-lib.mjs:ownerText` — a label becomes a synthetic text only when the record is built; scenarios `SC-AK-1456`, `SC-AK-1459`
