# Binding — the task flow as an executable scenario

A statement of the spec and the place where it is carried out. The link goes by the text of the
statement: a removed statement is removed together with its line.

- **The engine reads the description that lies next to it and names the states of the scenario in their order.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/task-flow-script.mjs:flowPath` — next to the laid-out engine or in the package, `--flow` names another; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/task-flow-script.mjs:stateLines` — a line per state with its action and pattern
- **A factory of a source or a measurer that is not written yet throws, so an unmeasured exit is never read as met.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/source/session.mjs:open`, `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/host.mjs:silent`, `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/owner.mjs:saidStop`, `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/session.mjs:wrote` — every factory of the package description not written yet; each is written by the step that measures its exit
- **The history of the branch is the commits the main branch on the server does not hold, and a fact read from it stays true in later states.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/source/git.mjs:open` — the commits, the version of a file at a commit, the last version and the commit that added a file; scenarios `SC-AK-1365`, `SC-AK-1378`
- **The parsing of the steps reaches a measurer through a source, by the module every reader of the steps shares.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/source/steps.mjs:checks` — the check line under each step of the plan, the steps by the shared parsing; scenarios `SC-AK-1369`, `SC-AK-1373`, `SC-AK-1375`
- **Exit 1 is met by a grill of the branch or of a draft that holds the owner's request as a quote and an answer.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/grill.mjs:written` — the grill of the branch from the working copy or the history, the drafts from the working copy; `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/source/tree.mjs:open` — the working copy as it lies on disk; `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/text.mjs:grillAnswered` — the two sections and their content; scenarios `SC-AK-1365`, `SC-AK-1366`
- **Exit 3 is met by a line of the grill or the plan naming the agreement directory found on disk or in the history, or the reason there is none.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/agreement.mjs:named` — both files, both sides of the history; `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/text.mjs:agreementLines` — the five forms of the line; scenarios `SC-AK-1367`, `SC-AK-1368`
- **Exit 5 is met by a plan the branch added whose every step has a check of a known form, and by an approval mark whose answer begins with «Одобряю».** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/plan.mjs:approved` — the plan as its commit wrote it, the forms of `expect`, the mark; a branch of `words.legacyBranches` needs no mark; scenarios `SC-AK-1369`, `SC-AK-1370`, `SC-AK-1372`
- **The marks lie in the common directory of git, and a mark that does not parse reads as absent.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/source/marks.mjs:marksDir` — the directory under the common directory, seen by every working copy; scenario `SC-AK-1371`
- **Exit 6 is met by a version of the progress in the commit of the plan or later that repeats its steps and marks exactly one of them as going on.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/progress.mjs:mirrors` — every version from the commit of the plan on; `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/text.mjs:sameSteps` — numbers and names in order; scenario `SC-AK-1373`
- **Exit 7 is met when every step is done and every done step has a green mark, and a plan without check lines is judged by the marks of the progress alone.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/progress.mjs:done` — the working copy, and the history without the folder; scenarios `SC-AK-1374`, `SC-AK-1375`
- **Exit 8 is met when every path of every edited footprint row has a changed file after the plan and no agreement directory under proposed/ stands in the head.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/footprint.mjs:touched` — the changes after the commit of the plan; `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/text.mjs:footprintRows` — the rows and their paths; scenarios `SC-AK-1376`, `SC-AK-1377`
- **Exit 9 is met by one commit that removed the task folder and added a record to the archive.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/folder.mjs:archived` — the deletion and the record in one commit; scenario `SC-AK-1378`
- **A read of the hosting is repeated once, and failed twice it is named as the silent hosting.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/source/host.mjs:attempt` — the repeat and the mark of silence; scenario `SC-AK-1384`
- **A met exit read from the hosting is kept for the life of the branch, and an answer for the later exits lives sixty seconds.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/source/host.mjs:timedRead` — the answer by the clock, the moment taken as a parameter; `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/source/host.mjs:createStore` — the answers in one file, dropped by a reset; scenarios `SC-AK-1381`, `SC-AK-1382`, `SC-AK-1383`
- **The measured state is the first exit known to be unmet; an exit the hosting could not answer is passed over, and with no unmet exit it releases the turn with its reason.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/task-flow-script.mjs:measureStates` — the order of the states, the unknown exits and the reason; scenarios `SC-AK-1385`, `SC-AK-1386`
- **The engine measures the checked-out branch by the measurers of the description over its sources and prints the state, its action and the exits read, as lines or as one JSON object.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/task-flow-script.mjs:openReaders` — the modules loaded first, a source opened when a measurer first asks for it; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/task-flow-script.mjs:measureLines` — the state, the action and the exits; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/task-flow-script.mjs:measureJson` — the same as one object; scenarios `SC-AK-1400`, `SC-AK-1401`
- **A branch past a state is not measured below it: a taken-apart folder, an epic merged into the main branch and an epic branch removed on the server keep the measured state.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/source/git.mjs:open` — the facts of the history; `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/epic.mjs:declared` — exit 2 kept once met; scenarios `SC-AK-1400`, `SC-AK-1401`, `SC-AK-1402`
- **A step is measured by the check the commit of the plan wrote; a matched step writes its mark, and an unmatched one drops it.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-step.mjs:measureStep` — the plan by its commit, the run and the verdict; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-step.mjs:planOfBranch` — the commit, the text and the fingerprint of the plan; `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/source/marks.mjs:writeMark` — the mark in the common directory; scenarios `SC-AK-1403`, `SC-AK-1404`, `SC-AK-1409`
- **A step is refused when its check is a command of the work, its expected result is in no form, or a file of the task folder differs from the commit of the plan.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-step.mjs:changedSincePlan` — every file the commit of the plan holds, the progress apart; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-step.mjs:StepRefused` — the refusal with its reason; scenarios `SC-AK-1406`, `SC-AK-1407`
- **A check runs with a limit of 540 seconds, and past it the step does not match.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-step.mjs:runCheck` — the limit taken as a parameter, `STEP_LIMIT_MS` at the boundary; scenario `SC-AK-1405`
- **The command for the done steps measures again every step the progress marks done.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-step.mjs:doneSteps` — the done steps of the working copy or of the history; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-step.mjs:runStepCommand` — a line per step and one exit code; scenario `SC-AK-1408`
- **A plan may be written only when every step carries a check of a known form that is no command of the work, and a form added to the description is known at once.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-plan.mjs:planProblems` — every step of the plan against `expect` and `words.write` of the description; scenarios `SC-AK-1410`, `SC-AK-1506`
- **The approval mark is written when every check is red before the work, both roles were called and the owner then chose an option that begins with «Одобряю» and names the plan or the task.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-plan.mjs:runPlanRed` — the runs, the red output and the mark; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-plan.mjs:approvingAnswer` — the roles and the menu answer after them in the record; scenarios `SC-AK-1411`, `SC-AK-1412`
- **A branch planned before the engine reads its plan from the newest commit that changed it.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-step.mjs:planOfBranch` — the closed list `words.legacyBranches` of the description; scenario `SC-AK-1539`
- **A check is replaced only by a decision in the progress whose quote stands in a message the owner typed.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-step.mjs:replacedCheck` — the line of the decision and the quote in the messages of the owner; scenario `SC-AK-1413`
- **The epic of the work is the one the task card names, and its plan lies in the plans directory.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/epic-of.mjs:epicOfWork` — the card of the task the branch carries, the plan by its number; `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/epic-text.mjs:epicPlan` — the number, the branch and the rows of the plan; scenarios `SC-AK-1379`, `SC-AK-1388`
