# Binding — the measures of the executable scenario

A statement of the spec and the place where it is carried out. The link goes by the text of the
statement: a removed statement is removed together with its line.

- **Nudges are counted per hundred calls by the pattern of the task flow description, read from the description itself.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/owner-nudges.mjs:measureNudges` — the typed messages the pattern matches and the calls; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/owner-nudges.mjs:flowPath` — `words.nudge` through the model of the description; scenario `SC-AK-1541`
- **A typed message is an entry the owner typed; a meta entry, a compaction summary, a tool result, a menu answer, a wrapper of the harness and a record of a subagent are none.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/owner-nudges.mjs:typedText` — on top of `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/source/owner.mjs:wordOf`; scenario `SC-AK-1542`
- **The records are read from the directories of every copy of the tree, and an entry repeated in a resumed session is counted once.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/owner-nudges.mjs:isCopyDir` — the main checkout and its worktrees; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/owner-nudges.mjs:entriesOf` — once by the id of the entry; scenario `SC-AK-1543`
- **Refusals are counted per hundred calls in four groups: all, the exit check, the checks of the base and the new checks.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/owner-nudges.mjs:refusalsOf` — the marks of a stop feedback and of a hook error; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/owner-nudges.mjs:measureRefusals` — the groups `EXIT_GUARDS`, `BASE_GUARDS`, `NEW_GUARDS`; scenario `SC-AK-1544`
- **A deadlock is a series of 10 and more stop refusals in one session with one guard and one first line of the reason; a stop that passed or another reason ends the series.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/owner-nudges.mjs:findDeadlocks` — the series by key; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/owner-nudges.mjs:stopPassed` — the summary of a stop without a refusal; scenario `SC-AK-1545`
- **The window is whole days of UTC ending with the day the caller names, and the counter never reads the clock inside a decision.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/owner-nudges.mjs:windowOf` — the moment comes from `--until`; the clock is read only as the default of the command line; scenario `SC-AK-1546`
- **A turn ends at a stop summary with no refusal in it and no stop feedback right after it.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-forecast.mjs:isTurnEnd` — the summary and the next entry of the session; scenario `SC-AK-1552`
- **Missed stops are counted in the last calls after the layout, only on branches that carry the engine.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/owner-nudges.mjs:lastCallsFrom` — the first of the last calls; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/owner-nudges.mjs:branchLaid` — the engine in the branch; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-forecast.mjs:runMissed` — the replay of the release cases; scenarios `SC-AK-1552`, `SC-AK-1553`
