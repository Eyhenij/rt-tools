# Binding — the guard of the exits of a turn

A statement of the spec and the place where it is carried out. The link goes by the text of the
statement: a removed statement is removed together with its line.

- **A turn ends with the scenario: the engine measures the branch, and a turn whose scenario is not over is held.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/hooks/turn-exit-guard.sh:verdict` — the input handed to the engine and its verdict turned into the answer; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/task-flow-script.mjs:runVerdict` — the measurement and the release cases; scenario `SC-AK-1427`

- **The owner's stop word releases the turn: their last word begins with a stop word of the description, and nothing after it asks to go on.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/owner.mjs:saidStop` — the owner's last word; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-data.mjs:saysStop` — the words of `words.stop`, `words.stopExcept` and `words.holdAfter`; `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/flow.js:stopWord` — the release case, second in order; scenarios `SC-AK-1912`, `SC-AK-1467`
- **The owner's interrupt releases the turn: their last word is the mark of Esc.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/owner.mjs:interrupted` — the owner's last word against `INTERRUPT`; `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/flow.js:interrupted` — the release case, third in order; scenario `SC-AK-1839`
- **A session that wrote nothing to the tree releases the turn unless the owner's last word pushes the work on, and a branch outside the scenario releases it always.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/session.mjs:wrote` — an edit or a command of the work; `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/owner.mjs:nudged` — `words.nudge` or a named task; `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-data.mjs:commandHead` — a command read past the variables and the options of git; scenarios `SC-AK-1432`, `SC-AK-1433`, `SC-AK-1440`, `SC-AK-1465`, `SC-AK-1555`
- **A role session with no edit tool ends with its report.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/hooks/turn-exit-role.sh:rt_te_read_only_role` — the role header before the engine is called; `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/session.mjs:role` — the same case in the description; scenario `SC-AK-1434`
- **The silent hosting releases the turn only when the measurement itself stopped on the silence.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/checks/flow-verdict.mjs:SILENCE` — the measurer read from where the measurement stopped; `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/host.mjs:silent` — a read failed twice; scenario `SC-AK-1435`
- **A menu of a refusal that did not reach the owner a second time in a row releases the turn.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/measure/session.mjs:menuLost` — the refusals with the menu and the menus after them; `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/source/session.mjs:eventsOf` — the events of the record; scenario `SC-AK-1444`

- **A refusal of a stop names the hook after «BLOCKED by» in its first line; after the opening of the model check only a name right after it counts, else the model check; otherwise none.** — `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/source/session.mjs:refusalBy` — the first line after the feedback header. `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/source/session.mjs:MODEL_CHECK` — the model check of a stop. `projects/agent-kit/assets/task-flow/source/session.mjs:eventsOf` — the field `by` of a refusal.
