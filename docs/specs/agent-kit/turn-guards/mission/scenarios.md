# Scenarios: the mission of the guards of the end of a turn

A subdomain of the domain "The guards of the end of a turn". It has no scenarios of its own: a rule
here is carried by a tool, and the scenarios of that tool live where the tool is described. The
command tiers are covered in the subdomains of the exits of a turn and in the domain itself; the
scenarios of the agent hooks are in the domain itself and are marked as not covered, because a
model is not run by the suite.

The cases of stops in the spec are not scenarios. They are the turns a new tool is replayed on
before it takes the place of the old one, by the order of the section "Replacing the tool".
