# What it is carried out by — the header of a bottom sheet and the cards of choice

The rule of the subdomain is on the left, the place where it is carried out is on the right. The
paths are given from the root of the tree.

- **Every sheet of the kit has a close button on the left of its header.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.html:bottom-sheet-close`, pressed into `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:dismiss`; the focus goes back by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:#returnFocus` to the node kept by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.logic.ts:outerFocus`; a sheet with its own header is named by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:ariaLabel`
- **The action of a sheet stands on the right of its header as a filled button.** — the slot `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.html:sheetAction`; a panel of choice fills it by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/popover/rt-popover-sheet-options.directive.ts:rtPopoverSheetAction`, passed on by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/popover/rt-popover-sheet.helper.ts:bindSheetOptions`. The done button is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/multiselect/rt-multiselect.component.html:sheetDoneTpl` with `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/multiselect/rt-multiselect.component.ts:closeSheet`, the apply button of the dynamic selector is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/dynamic-selector/rt-dynamic-selector.component.ts:onSheetApply`. The date sheets: `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/date-picker/rt-date-picker.component.html:date-picker-sheet-apply` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/date-range/rt-date-range.component.html:date-range-sheet-apply`, the footer button taken off by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/date-picker/panel/rt-date-panel.component.ts:applyOutside` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/date-range/panel/rt-date-range-panel.component.ts:applyOutside`
- **A list of choice in the sheet is drawn as cards.** — the input `sheetCards` and the computed `cards` of each part: `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/select/rt-select.component.ts:sheetCards`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/multiselect/rt-multiselect.component.ts:sheetCards`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/autocomplete/rt-autocomplete.component.ts:sheetCards`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/dynamic-selector/rt-dynamic-selector.component.ts:sheetCards`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/table/filter-header/rt-table-filter-header.component.ts:sheetCards`. The look of a card is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/styles/_mixins.scss:sheet-card`, the list of cards is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/styles/_mixins.scss:sheet-card-list`, the fill under the pointer is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/styles/_mixins.scss:sheet-card-hover` and under the keyboard position `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/styles/_mixins.scss:sheet-card-active`, with the handles `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/styles/_mixins.scss:--rt-sheet-card-bg-hover`; the card of the kit checkbox is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/checkbox/rt-checkbox.component.ts:card`; the mark of a hint is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/autocomplete/rt-autocomplete.component.html:autocomplete-option-mark`; the look held while the sheet slides away is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/popover/rt-popover.directive.ts:shownInSheet`
- **An option carries an optional description.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/options/rt-option-tree.model.ts:description`, shown in the card of the checkbox by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/checkbox/rt-checkbox.component.ts:description`
- **The time in a sheet is chosen by a wheel.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/date-picker/panel/rt-date-wheel.directive.ts:RtDateWheelDirective`, which value stands under the band — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/date-picker/rt-date-wheel.logic.ts:rtDateWheelIndex`, the look — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/date-picker/panel/rt-date-wheel.scss:rt-date-panel--sheet`; the turn without an end — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/date-picker/rt-date-wheel.logic.ts:rtDateWheelRows` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/date-picker/rt-date-wheel.logic.ts:rtDateWheelHome`; the start time — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/date-picker/rt-date-panel.logic.ts:rtDateStartTime`, chosen by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/date-picker/rt-date-picker.component.ts:timeStart`; the keys and the resting finger — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/date-picker/panel/rt-date-wheel.directive.ts:onStep` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/date-picker/panel/rt-date-wheel.directive.ts:onTouch`
- **A sheet opens by the height of its content and takes the full height while the screen keyboard is shown.** — the place above the keyboard — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet-keyboard.ts:rtWatchSheetKeyboard`; the full height while it is shown — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:raised`; the watch — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:typing`, set on the focus by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.logic.ts:isTypingTarget`; the height of a touch screen is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.scss:pointer: coarse`. The switch of the application is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:fullHeight`, for a panel of choice `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/popover/rt-popover-sheet-options.directive.ts:rtPopoverSheetFull`
- **A sheet whose choice reaches the application only by a button asks before it closes with a choice not applied.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:unsaved`, the answers go by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:onConfirmApply` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:applyRequest`, the dropped input by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:discardRequest` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/page-header/bar/rt-page-bar.component.ts:filtersDiscard`, the focus of the question by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:confirmFirst`; the date sheets ask by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/date-picker/panel/rt-date-panel.component.ts:dirty` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/date-range/panel/rt-date-range-panel.component.ts:dirty`. A panel of choice passes it by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/popover/rt-popover-sheet-options.directive.ts:rtPopoverSheetUnsaved`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/popover/rt-popover-sheet-options.directive.ts:rtPopoverSheetApply` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/popover/rt-popover-sheet.helper.ts:rtPopoverSheetDismissals`. The marks of the dynamic selector are `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/dynamic-selector/rt-dynamic-selector.component.ts:sheetUnsaved` over `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/dynamic-selector/rt-dynamic-selector.component.ts:pendingKeys` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/dynamic-selector/rt-dynamic-selector-parts.service.ts:ticked`. The input of the page filters is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/page-header/bar/rt-page-bar.component.ts:sheetUnsaved` over `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/page-header/bar/rt-page-bar.component.ts:sheetTouched` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/page-header/bar/rt-page-bar.component.ts:filtersChanged`
- **Every sheet locks the scroll of the page and closes by Escape.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:#setScrollLock` with the styles loaded by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:#overlayContainer`, the key inside a date panel by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/date-picker/panel/rt-date-panel.component.ts:onEscape`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:onEscape` and, for the key pressed inside the sheet, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:onHostEscape`, which asks to close by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:closeByEscape`; the key caught by the overlay of a panel of choice comes the same way through `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/popover/rt-popover-sheet.component.ts:closeByEscape`
