# What it is carried out by — the header of a bottom sheet and the cards of choice

The rule of the subdomain is on the left, the place where it is carried out is on the right. The
paths are given from the root of the tree.

- **Every sheet of the kit has a close button on the left of its header.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.html:bottom-sheet-close`, pressed into `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:dismiss`
- **The action of a sheet stands on the right of its header as a filled button.** — the slot `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.html:sheetAction`; a panel of choice fills it by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/popover/rt-popover-sheet-options.directive.ts:rtPopoverSheetAction`, passed on by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/popover/rt-popover-sheet.helper.ts:bindSheetOptions`. The done button is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/multiselect/rt-multiselect.component.html:sheetDoneTpl` with `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/multiselect/rt-multiselect.component.ts:closeSheet`, the apply button of the dynamic selector is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/dynamic-selector/rt-dynamic-selector.component.ts:onSheetApply`
- **A list of choice in the sheet is drawn as cards.** — the input `sheetCards` and the computed `cards` of each part: `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/select/rt-select.component.ts:sheetCards`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/multiselect/rt-multiselect.component.ts:sheetCards`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/autocomplete/rt-autocomplete.component.ts:sheetCards`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/dynamic-selector/rt-dynamic-selector.component.ts:sheetCards`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/table/filter-header/rt-table-filter-header.component.ts:sheetCards`. The look of a card is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/styles/_mixins.scss:sheet-card`, the list of cards is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/styles/_mixins.scss:sheet-card-list`; the card of the kit checkbox is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/checkbox/rt-checkbox.component.ts:card`
- **An option carries an optional description.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/options/rt-option-tree.model.ts:description`, shown in the card of the checkbox by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/checkbox/rt-checkbox.component.ts:description`
- **A sheet with a search opens by the height of its content and takes the full height while the focus is in its search.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/select/rt-select.component.ts:searchFocused`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/multiselect/rt-multiselect.component.ts:searchFocused`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/autocomplete/rt-autocomplete.component.ts:searchFocused`, passed to the sheet by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/popover/rt-popover-sheet-options.directive.ts:rtPopoverSheetFull` into `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:fullHeight`
- **The dynamic selector, the page filters and the filters of a thread list open at full height.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/dynamic-selector/rt-dynamic-selector.component.html:rtPopoverSheetFull`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/page-header/bar/rt-page-bar.component.html:fullHeight`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/thread-list/rt-thread-list.component.html:rtPopoverSheetFull`
- **A sheet whose choice reaches the application only by a button asks before it closes with a choice not applied.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:unsaved`, the answers go by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:onConfirmApply` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:applyRequest`. A panel of choice passes it by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/popover/rt-popover-sheet-options.directive.ts:rtPopoverSheetUnsaved`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/popover/rt-popover-sheet-options.directive.ts:rtPopoverSheetApply` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/popover/rt-popover-sheet.helper.ts:rtPopoverSheetDismissals`. The marks of the dynamic selector are `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/dynamic-selector/rt-dynamic-selector.component.ts:sheetUnsaved` over `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/dynamic-selector/rt-dynamic-selector.component.ts:pendingKeys` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/dynamic-selector/rt-dynamic-selector-parts.service.ts:ticked`. The input of the page filters is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/page-header/bar/rt-page-bar.component.ts:sheetUnsaved` over `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/page-header/bar/rt-page-bar.component.ts:sheetTouched` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/page-header/bar/rt-page-bar.component.ts:filtersChanged`
- **Every sheet locks the scroll of the page and closes by Escape.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:#setScrollLock`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:onEscape`, which asks to close by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:closeByEscape`; the key caught by the overlay of a panel of choice comes the same way through `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/popover/rt-popover-sheet.component.ts:closeByEscape`
- **With reduced motion asked by the system the sheet appears without sliding.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.scss:prefers-reduced-motion`

The scenarios of the subdomain are bound to the tests by the number in the title of a test, not by a
table here: the bond is checked both ways by the checking of the specs.
