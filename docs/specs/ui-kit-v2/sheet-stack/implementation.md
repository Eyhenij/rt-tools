# What it is carried out by — the sheet opened from a sheet

The first column is the rule of the spec next to it verbatim. The second is where it is carried out
in the tree, and the scenarios it is checked by; what exactly every scenario is covered by is said in
`scenarios.md`.

- **A sheet opened from a sheet opens in the same sheet.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:#nestOver` into `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet-screens.ts:nestOver` takes the height of the sheet below and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet-screens.ts:followUnder` follows it on a resize of the window, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.scss:&--nested`, the chevron `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.html:bottom-sheet-back`; the first sheet hides by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:covered`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.scss:--covered`; scenarios `SC-UKV-1631`, `SC-UKV-1867`
- **The second screen is never lower than the first one and grows by its content up to the limit of a full-height sheet.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.scss:&--nested` takes the least height from `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet-screens.ts:nestedHeight`, the limit is the one of `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.scss:&__panel`; scenario `SC-UKV-1867`
- **The switch between the screens and the change of height are animated, not a jump.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet-screens.ts:rtSwitchSheetScreen`, called by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet-screens.ts:switchFrom` on the opening of the second screen and by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet-screens.ts:leave` on its closing; scenario `SC-UKV-1867`
- **Closing the top sheet shows the previous one again.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet-stack.service.ts:remove`; scenario `SC-UKV-1632`
