# What it is carried out by — the side panel as a full-height sheet on a phone

The first column is the rule of the spec next to it verbatim. The second is where it is carried out
in the tree; the paths are given from the root of the tree. The scenarios are bound to the tests by
the number in the title of a test, not by a table here: the bond is checked both ways by the
checking of the specs.

- **On a phone every side panel opens as a bottom sheet of the full height.** — the form of one opening is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside-sheet.ts:RtAsideSheet`, given by the service through `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside-sheet.ts:RT_ASIDE_SHEET` with the overlay of `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside.service.ts:#sheetConfig`, and by the right panel of the frame through `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/container/rt-container-sheet.ts:RtContainerSheetDriver`; the panel draws the kit's sheet by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside.component.ts:sheetMode`
- **From 481 points a side panel stays as it is.** — the threshold `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/platform/breakpoints.service.ts:phone`; the panel at the side is `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside.service.ts:#sideConfig` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/container/rt-container-sheet.ts:leaveOverlay`
- **The form is chosen by the window at each opening and kept until the panel closes.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside-sheet.ts:activate`, called once per opening by the service and by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/container/rt-container-sheet.ts:choose`
- **The panel sheet takes the full height whatever its content.** — the input `fullHeight` of `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:fullHeight`, set by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside.component.html:fullHeight`; the place above the keyboard is the sheet's own, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet-keyboard.ts:rtWatchSheetKeyboard`
- **The panel sheet is as wide as the window and always dims the page.** — the overlay box `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/_rt-aside-overlay.scss:rt-aside-overlay--sheet`, put on it by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside.service.ts:#sheetConfig` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/container/rt-container-sheet.ts:openOverlay`; the dim is the sheet's backdrop, whatever the frame's input
- **The panel sheet slides up from the bottom and leaves sliding down.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside-sheet.ts:show`, raised by the service and by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/container/rt-container-sheet.ts:raise`, lowered by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside-ref.ts:close` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/container/rt-container-sheet.ts:lower`; reduced motion — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.scss:prefers-reduced-motion`
- **The panel sheet has the header of a sheet: the handle, «×» on the left, the title in the centre, the main action on the right.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/header/rt-aside-header.component.html:aside-sheet-close`, drawn while `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/header/rt-aside-header.component.ts:sheetMode`; the columns — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/header/rt-aside-header.component.scss:&__content--sheet`
- **The main action moves from the footer to the right of the header as a filled button.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside-main-action.ts:RtAsideMainAction` moves the node picked by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/footer/rt-aside-footer.component.ts:mainActionNode` into `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/header/rt-aside-header.component.ts:sheetMainSlot`; its look — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/footer/rt-aside-footer.component.ts:SHEET_MAIN_LOOK`
- **The icon buttons of the panel header stand to the left of the main action.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/header/rt-aside-header.component.html:aside-sheet-main`, the last node of the actions row
- **A close-only button is not drawn in the sheet, and the other buttons of the footer stay in its footer.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/footer/rt-aside-footer.component.ts:closeOnlyHidden`, the footer left empty by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/footer/rt-aside-footer.component.ts:sheetFilled`; the size of a button by its place — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/button/rt-button.directive.ts:placeLook`; the 12 px around the buttons — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.scss:&__footer`
- **The panel sheet closes by «×», by a swipe down past the threshold of a sheet, by a tap on the strip above it and by Escape.** — the gestures leave the sheet by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside.component.ts:onSheetOpenChange` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/header/rt-aside-header.component.ts:onSheetClose` as `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside-sheet.ts:requestClose`; the service answers by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside.service.ts:#answerSheet`, the frame by its `backdropClick`
- **A panel with an edits guard asks about its unsaved edits before it closes by any of the four roads and by a navigation.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/container/rt-route-aside.unsaved.ts:rtAskUnsaved`, called by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/container/rt-route-aside.base.ts:#askUnsaved` for the buttons and for the guard of the router
- **On a phone a panel opened by the service with the unsaved sign set asks the same question before it closes by a gesture or by «×».** — the sign `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside-ref.ts:unsaved` and the request `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside-ref.ts:saveRequests`, read by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside.service.ts:#answerSheet`
- **On a phone the question about the unsaved edits stands at the bottom of the screen in the look of the sheet's question.** — the words of the panel by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside.component.ts:sheetConfirm` into `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:confirmLabels`, the save answer that leaves the sheet open by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:closeOnApply`; the two cards — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.scss:&__confirm-card`
- **The filters of the toolbar in the sheet ask before they close with a change not applied.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/toolbar/filters-aside/rt-toolbar-filters-aside.component.ts:onFieldChange` sets the unsaved sign by values; the drop is reported by `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/toolbar/rt-toolbar.component.ts:filtersDiscard`
- **Escape closes the top layer only, wherever the focus stands.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:onEscape` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:onHostEscape`; the service and the frame leave Escape of a sheet to it in `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside.service.ts:RtAsideService`
- **The opened panel sheet puts the focus on its «×», unless the panel marked a node of its own for the first focus; the closed one returns it to the node it was opened from.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside.component.ts:#focusSheet`; the return — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:#returnFocus`
- **A sheet opened from a sheet opens in the same sheet: its content and title take the place of the first ones, and «×» on the left becomes a back chevron.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.ts:#nestOver`, the chevron `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/bottom-sheet/rt-bottom-sheet.component.html:bottom-sheet-back`
- **The parts of a panel live in the sheet unchanged.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside.component.html:aside-sheet`, the same frame of the panel inside the sheet; the meta under the title — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/header/rt-aside-header.component.html:aside-sheet-meta`
- **The tabs of a panel sheet are one row that scrolls round, like the presets of the period sheet.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside-sheet-tabs.ts:RtAsideSheetTabs`
- **The panel sheet is announced as a modal dialog by the name of the panel, else by its title.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside.component.ts:sheetName`
- **No setting keeps the side panel at the side on a phone.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/aside/rt-aside.service.ts:#phone` is the only thing the service reads to choose the form
