# What it is carried out by — the colour palette

The first column is the rule of the spec next to it verbatim. The second is where it is carried out
in the tree, and the scenario it is checked by.

- **The value is a colour `#rrggbb` in lower case; anything else written into the field reads as no colour.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/rt-color-palette.logic.ts:colorPaletteHex`; scenario `SC-UKV-807`
- **The swatches draw the given colours in their order, or the kit's eighteen when none are given, and a value outside them is drawn as one more swatch after them.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/rt-color-palette.logic.ts:colorPaletteSwatches`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/rt-color-palette.const.ts:RT_COLOR_PALETTE_DEFAULT`; scenario `SC-UKV-808`
- **A press on a swatch chooses its colour, and the field reports the change and the touch.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/rt-color-palette.component.ts:onSwatch`; scenario `SC-UKV-809`
- **The swatches are a radio group: one swatch is in the Tab order, the arrows choose the next and the previous, Home and End the ends.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/rt-color-palette.logic.ts:colorPaletteNextIndex`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/rt-color-palette.component.ts:onSwatchKeydown`; scenario `SC-UKV-810`
- **Read only draws one swatch of the current colour and nothing to press.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/rt-color-palette.component.ts:readonly`; scenario `SC-UKV-811`
- **A disabled field draws muted swatches and takes no press and no key.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/rt-color-palette.component.ts:onSwatch`; scenario `SC-UKV-812`
- **The picker opens from the custom colour button, starts from the current colour, and only Apply writes the picked colour into the field.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/rt-color-palette.component.ts:onCustomApply`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/picker/rt-color-picker.component.ts:applied`; scenario `SC-UKV-813`
- **In the picker the area takes saturation and brightness and the strip takes the hue, by the pointer and by the arrows; the hex field takes a typed colour once it is a full hex.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/rt-color-palette.logic.ts:colorPaletteStep`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/picker/rt-color-picker.component.ts:hexControl`; scenario `SC-UKV-814`
- **The channels input adds red, green and blue fields under the hex field; each shows its channel and takes a typed whole number from 0 to 255.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/rt-color-palette.logic.ts:colorPaletteChannel`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/picker/rt-color-picker.component.ts:channelControls`; scenario `SC-UKV-847`
- **Every size, gap, ring, border and radius of the palette and the picker is a `--rt-color-palette-*` property, and without them the palette draws the kit look.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/rt-color-palette.component.scss:rt-color-palette-swatch-size`; scenario `SC-UKV-815`
- **The material preset draws the application's look by the same properties, without Material packages: borderless swatches of 30 points with gaps of 10, a hollow ring on the chosen one, and a swatch that grows under the pointer.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/rt-color-palette.component.scss:rt-color-palette-checked-hollow`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/rt-color-palette.component.scss:rt-color-palette-custom-gap`, `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/color-palette/picker/rt-color-picker.component.scss:rt-color-palette-picker-height`; scenario `SC-UKV-815`
