# What it is carried out by — the assistant chat

The first column is the rule of the spec next to it verbatim. The second is where it is carried out
in the tree; the scenario it is checked by is named there too, and what exactly every scenario is
covered by is said in `scenarios.md`.

- **An empty conversation shows the greeting and the suggestion cards, and a pressed card sends its text.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/rt-ai-chat.component.ts:onSuggestion` — `isEmpty` draws `rt-empty-state` and the `rt-prompt-suggestion` cards. Scenario `SC-UKV-750`
- **A question stands on the right in a bubble with «You», an answer on the left as markdown text.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/rt-ai-chat.component.ts:messages` — the template draws the `question` element for the role `user` and `rt-ai-chat-answer` for the rest. Scenario `SC-UKV-751`
- **The run line stands above the answer text, and its steps open for one answer at a time.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/rt-ai-chat.component.ts:openRuns` — `onToggleRun` keeps the ids of opened answers. Scenario `SC-UKV-752`
- **The rating appears once the answer text is written, and pressing the chosen rating again removes it.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/answer/rt-ai-chat-answer.component.ts:isWritten` and `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/rt-ai-chat.component.ts:onFeedback`. Scenario `SC-UKV-753`
- **A written answer has «Copy» first in its rating row, and the press puts the answer text as the reader sees it, without markdown signs, into the clipboard and shows «Copied» for two seconds.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/answer/rt-ai-chat-answer.component.ts:isWritten` draws `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/copy/rt-ai-chat-copy.component.ts:onCopy` first in the row; `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/answer/rt-ai-chat-answer.component.ts:plainText` gives it the text through `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/util/markdown-plain-text.ts:markdownToPlainText`. Scenario `SC-UKV-777`
- **A question has «Copy» in a row under its bubble at the bubble's side, it copies the question text, and the consumer can turn both copy buttons off.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/rt-ai-chat.component.ts:copyable` — the template draws the `question-actions` element under the bubble. Scenario `SC-UKV-778`
- **While an answer is written, the composer offers Stop, and the suggestions and «New conversation» are off.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/rt-ai-chat.component.ts:sending` — the composer is `stoppable`. Scenario `SC-UKV-754`
- **A run error shows the message, the reference number with a copy button, and «Ask again» only when the answer can be asked again.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/rt-ai-chat.component.ts:error` — the template draws `rt-message`, `rt-copy-value` for `referenceId` and the retry button for `retryable`. Scenario `SC-UKV-755`
- **The feed follows the last answer while the person stays at the bottom, and stops when they scroll up.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/rt-ai-chat.component.ts:onFeedScroll` — `nextAtBottom` of the chat thread logic and `RT_CHAT_PIN_RETRY_DELAYS_MS`. Shown by the frame `Long`
- **In a narrow panel the conversations open in place of the feed, on full screen they stand as a column on the left, and choosing one returns to the feed.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/rt-ai-chat.component.ts:isThreadPage`, `isThreadColumn`, `onSelectThread`. Scenario `SC-UKV-756`
- **The search over conversations marks every match, and an empty result says «Nothing found» instead of «No conversations yet».** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/rt-ai-chat.component.ts:threadRows` — `splitSideMenuTitle`; `threadsEmptyText`. Scenario `SC-UKV-757`
- **Deleting a conversation is an action of its row and does not open the conversation.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/thread-list/rt-thread-list.directives.ts:RtThreadListRowActionsDirective`. Scenario `SC-UKV-758`
- **Focus follows the person: the open list of conversations puts it on «Back to conversation», and choosing a conversation, a new conversation, Stop and the return to the feed put it into the composer.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/rt-ai-chat.component.ts:focusComposer` — `onToggleThreads`, `onSelectThread`, `onNewThread`, `onStop` call it after the render. Scenario `SC-UKV-761`
- **The attachments of an answer come from the consumer's template, which receives the message.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/rt-ai-chat.directives.ts:RtAiChatMessageExtraDirective`. Scenario `SC-UKV-759`
- **The title, the placeholder, the empty-screen texts and the line under the composer come from the consumer, and the kit's English text stands in when one is not given.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/rt-ai-chat.component.ts:titleText`, `disclaimerText`. Scenario `SC-UKV-760`
- **The empty list of conversations draws preview rows with the assistant's marks, and the consumer sets its own icons.** — `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/rich-editor/lib/components/ai-chat/rt-ai-chat.component.ts:emptyPreviewIcons` passes the icons into `projects/ui-kit-v2/src/lib/components/thread-list/rt-thread-list.component.ts:emptyPreviewRows`, which draws a row per icon and shifts every second one. Scenarios `SC-UKV-775`, `SC-UKV-776`
